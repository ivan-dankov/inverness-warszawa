## Age and ear piercing – what to choose?

The choice of the right age depends on individual family preferences:

### Babies (0-12 months)

**Advantages:**
- Child does not remember the discomfort
- Parents have full control over care
- Faster healing thanks to young tissues
- Lower risk of earring being pulled out

**Best for:** Parents who want to traditionally pierce ears at an early age and are ready for systematic care.

### Young children (1-5 years)

**Advantages:**
- Children can express desire to have earrings
- Still fast healing
- Parents control the care process

**Best for:** Parents who want to wait until the child shows interest, but still maintain control over care.

### Older children (6+ years)

**Advantages:**
- Child's conscious decision
- Can take care of hygiene themselves (under supervision)
- Lower risk of losing earrings
- Can choose preferred design

**Best for:** Parents who want the child to make a conscious decision themselves and participate in care.

## When NOT to pierce ears – contraindications

Regardless of the chosen age, there are situations in which the procedure should be postponed:

- Active skin infections or eczema in the ear area
- Fever, cold or body weakness
- Directly after vaccinations (wait 2-3 days)
- Blood clotting disorders (consult a doctor)
- Strong confirmed metal allergy

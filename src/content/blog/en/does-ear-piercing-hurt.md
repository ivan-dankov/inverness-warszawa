### Why is Inverness Med Less Painful?

## How Long Do Ears Take to Heal After Piercing?

Healing time depends on the piercing location:

- **Earlobe**: 6-8 weeks – fastest healing
- **Cartilage**: 3-6 months – requires longer care

During this time, it's important to:
- Avoid sleeping on the pierced ear
- Regularly clean the procedure site
- Not touch earrings with dirty hands
- Avoid pools and saunas until healed

## Most Common Problems After Piercing and How to Avoid Them

### Light Redness or Swelling

This is a normal body reaction to piercing and usually disappears within the first 24-48 hours. To reduce swelling:
- Apply a cold compress for 10-15 minutes several times a day
- Keep your head elevated during sleep
- Continue regular cleaning

### Severe Pain or Discharge

If severe pain, pus discharge, or prolonged redness occurs, this may indicate an infection. In such a case:
- Immediately contact a professional salon or doctor
- Do not remove the earring yourself
- Continue cleaning according to instructions

### Allergic Reaction

Although Inverness earrings are made of hypoallergenic materials, in rare cases an allergic reaction may occur. Symptoms include:
- Severe itching
- Prolonged redness
- Rash around the piercing

In such a case, contact a specialist to replace the earring with a material of higher hypoallergenicity (titanium or niobium).

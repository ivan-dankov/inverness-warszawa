Many people who decide to get their ears pierced for the first time wonder not only if the procedure hurts, but also **which piercing method to choose**. There are several methods available on the market – from traditional guns to modern, medical systems like [Inverness Med](https://www.invernesscorp.com/). Each method differs in precision, hygiene level and comfort, so it's worth learning about their advantages and disadvantages before making a decision.

## Different piercing methods – which one to choose?

If you want a gentle, safe and painless piercing – choose **Inverness Med**.

## Hygiene and safety are fundamental

In the Inverness Med system, every element is sterile and disposable, which practically eliminates the risk of infection. After the procedure, you should:

- wash your hands before touching the ear,
- clean the piercing site 2–3 times a day with a special solution ([aftercare](/en/aftercare)),
- avoid alcohol and irritating cosmetics,
- do not remove earrings before healing.

## Does ear piercing really hurt?

Most people compare it to a light mosquito bite. The Inverness Med system works quietly and gently – without a shot, like in guns. Discomfort is minimal and lasts only a few seconds.

## How long do ears take to heal after piercing?

- Earlobe – 6–8 weeks,
- Cartilage – 3–6 months.

During this time, you should avoid sleeping on the pierced ear and regularly clean the procedure site. Inverness earrings are made of hypoallergenic materials – surgical steel, titanium and niobium (see **earrings**).

## Most common problems after piercing

Slight redness or swelling is natural. If severe pain or discharge occurs, you should contact a professional salon or doctor. Proper hygiene and care will help avoid this (more in [aftercare](/en/aftercare)).

## Ear piercing for children

The Inverness Med system is certified for children from 0+, which means you can safely pierce ears even for infants. The procedure is quick and gentle, and the child feels minimal discomfort.

## Summary

Ear piercing with the Inverness Med system is a safe, sterile and practically painless procedure.

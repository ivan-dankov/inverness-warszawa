In Warsaw, two methods of ear piercing are most commonly used: **traditional piercing gun** and **medical Inverness Med system (USA)**. The difference between them determines the level of safety, sterility, precision and healing speed.

## What is the Inverness Med Medical System

**Inverness Med** is a modern system for sterile ear piercing. It works on the principle of **smooth manual pressure**, not an impact mechanism, making the piercing maximally gentle.

### Main advantages of Inverness Med:

- silent piercing
- gentle movement without tissue tearing
- sterile disposable cartridges
- laser-sharpened needle
- hypoallergenic materials
- medical FDA and REACH certificates
- Safety Back lock that does not press the earring against the skin

## How a regular piercing gun works

A piercing gun performs the piercing through a **strong impact** that "shoots" the earring into the tissue. This mechanism uses a spring that creates a sudden push. Unlike Inverness Med, a piercing gun cannot be fully sterilized between procedures because the internal mechanisms are difficult to clean.

### Disadvantages of piercing gun:

- greater trauma
- loud sound → stress for children
- internal elements are not sterilized
- risk of cross-contamination
- less precision
- slower healing

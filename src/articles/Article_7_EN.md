# How Much Does Ear Piercing Cost in Warsaw? [Complete Price Guide 2026]

The question about ear piercing prices seems simple. But most places in Warsaw only show part of the picture – just the service without earrings, or a package without explaining what you actually get.

Prices in Warsaw range from 80 PLN (gun at beauty salons) to 600 PLN (professional needle studios). The cheapest option often ends up more expensive than premium – through complications, earring replacements, or prolonged healing.

This guide shows the full costs: service + earrings + aftercare + potential complications. No hidden fees.

## What's Included in Ear Piercing Costs?

Every ear piercing has four things you pay for:

**Service** (50-220 PLN) – which system they use (gun, needle, Inverness Med), who performs it, studio visit or home service.

**Earrings** (30-180 PLN) – material (cheap steel, medical steel, titanium, gold), sterility (hand-touched vs sealed cassette), clasp type (butterfly vs Safety Back).

**Aftercare** (20-50 PLN) – antiseptic solution for 6-8 weeks of healing, care instructions.

**Potential complications** (0-300 PLN) – if something goes wrong. Infection means doctor visit (100-200 PLN). Prolonged healing means more solution (20-40 PLN). Allergy means earring replacement (50-180 PLN).

Example: cheap option (80 PLN) + complications (150 PLN) = 230 PLN. Premium option (270 PLN) without problems = 270 PLN. Difference 40 PLN, but one heals in 12 weeks, the other in 6 weeks.
